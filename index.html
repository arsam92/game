    IN WAR, NO ONE WINS.
    a single-file survival drama — everything you see and hear is drawn and synthesized by your own browser

    HOW TO SURVIVE

    MORNING. Assign one task to each survivor: rest, repair the house, search downstairs for food, craft tools, care for the wounded, or stand guard. Guards make night raids far less likely.

    DUSK. Supper, and then the city knocks. Every event offers choices. Choices are immediate and irreversible — people remember, and the city remembers.

    NIGHT. Send one survivor out to a location: an abandoned house, the hospital, the checkpoint, or a family home that is not empty. Every place hides loot, witnesses and prices. The one who stays home might face a raid.

    NEEDS. Health at 0 is death, and death is permanent. Hunger above 80 becomes illness. Morale under 20 becomes depression, and depression makes people useless.

    RESOURCES. Food, medicine, parts (salvage), tools (crafted from parts) and the shelter itself (repaired with parts, 0–5).

    THE STORY. Five threads weave through the siege: a hidden hospital that heals at a price, a lost child, a neighbor who informs, a missing sister, and the last train out on day 30. Pull one thread and the others move.

    DEATHS & ENDINGS. The game autosaves at every phase. Nobody dies instantly from one mistake — but everything adds up. There is more than one way out, and more than one way to survive.

    Inspired by the siege of a city that could be any city. In war, no one wins.
